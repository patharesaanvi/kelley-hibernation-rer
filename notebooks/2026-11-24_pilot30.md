# Lab notebook — November 24, 2026: 30-gene pilot

## Objective
Scale the earlier single-gene HBB workflow to a small multi-gene test, determine whether the resulting gene trees can be imported together into RERconverge, and test whether relative evolutionary-rate residuals can be calculated.

## Progress made
- Used a **30-gene pilot** selected from the existing larger candidate list rather than running all 50 candidates immediately.
- Adapted the previously developed coding-exon retrieval and tree-fitting workflow to multiple genes, using the **Hiller hg38 470-way mammalian alignment** and human **MANE coding-exon intervals**.
- Locally staged **153 nonempty coding-exon files** across 30 genes. Staging and later computation were separated because accessing UCSC from compute nodes had encountered network timeouts.
- Applied a **10% missing-nucleotide filter** and fitted gene-specific branch lengths with **RAxML-NG** on a fixed mammalian species-tree topology. An initial **minimum of 35 species** excluded TRIM52 (30 species); it was lowered to **4 solely for the technical pilot**. In the final 30-gene set, each gene actually retained **30–53 species**.
- All **30 gene-tree jobs succeeded**. The first five checked were ANKRD49 (52 species; 717 bp), INHBB (38; 1221 bp), IYD (50; 867 bp), TMEM126B (50; 690 bp), and TRIM52 (30; 861 bp).
- Three additional trees, **ADNP, SAMD8, RELL2**, retained all 53 species. The union of tips across all trees contained 53 species; 10 species were common to all 30 trees.
- Combined the 30 gene trees and checked compatible nontrivial **unrooted splits** against the pruned full-coverage ADNP topology; checked that fitted branch lengths had no missing, negative, or nonfinite values. These checks are structural, not independent evidence that every alignment or ortholog is correct.
- Successfully ran **RERconverge `readTrees()`** for all 30 gene trees. The master tree contains 53 taxa and 103 edges; the master-tree branch lengths were estimated from the three full-coverage genes because `minTreesAll=3` was used.
- Successfully ran **`getAllResiduals()`**, unlike the earlier HBB-only attempt, generating a preliminary residual matrix containing **30 genes and 627 phylogenetic-path columns**. The next step is to understand how these paths are represented and validate the results before interpreting evolutionary-rate differences.
- **Committed the core 30-gene pilot workflow to GitHub:** three Python scripts (`prepare_pilot.py`, `stage_thirty.py`, `extract_and_fit.py`), two RERconverge R scripts (`test_rer30.R`, `calculate_rer30.R`), five SLURM submission scripts, and the three candidate-gene tables.
- **Updated the five SLURM wrappers** to invoke scripts from the GitHub repository's `scripts/` directory without changing analysis parameters. The updated wrappers still need an independent execution check on Hummingbird.
- **Added explanatory comments** throughout the newly committed Python, R, and SLURM scripts, describing inputs, outputs, major processing steps, and key parameters. Updated the workflow documentation to reflect the committed files and current validation status.
